## Design Context

### Users
This is a local, personal investment research workstation for one primary user. The user runs data updates, checks data quality, screens symbols, backtests simple strategies, and reads traceable research reports on a private machine.

### Brand Personality
Calm, credible, restrained. The interface should feel like a serious research desk: dense enough for repeated work, clear enough to audit, and explicit about uncertainty.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use a light, editorial workstation style with warm paper neutrals, ink-like text, and restrained market-state accents. Avoid marketing-page heroes, decorative gradients, dark neon dashboards, and card-heavy landing-page composition.

### Design Principles
- Put the active workflow on the first screen; do not create a landing page.
- Make run traceability visible: command, exit code, run_id, snapshot_id, config_hash, and quality state.
- Keep controls compact and predictable; favor forms, tables, and status bands over decorative cards.
- Distinguish research output from investment advice in every primary view.
- Show empty states that explain the next local command instead of pretending data exists.
